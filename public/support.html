Tangible support

See the difference a contribution can make

Support can contribute to these areas of work. GirlsCARE will confirm current needs and contribution arrangements directly; these are not fixed-price packages.

Grow climate leadership

Mentorship, training, and opportunities for girls and young women to participate in climate justice.

Support recovery and care

Practical support, education, and wellbeing for girls and communities affected by climate impacts.

Strengthen the organisation

Resources for programme delivery, collaboration, and longer-term organisational development.

Ways to contribute

Support can take more than one form

GirlsCARE can use flexible support as well as resources tied to a specific programme, activity, or community need.

01

Fund the work

Contribute to mentorship, climate learning, regional engagement, relief response, or the systems that keep programmes running.

02

Sponsor a practical need

Help cover materials, transport, participant access, event costs, or a clearly defined piece of programme delivery.

03

Offer in-kind support

Provide relevant supplies, venues, professional expertise, technology, or services that meet an agreed programme need.

04

Build a partnership

Explore longer-term funding, co-designed programmes, research, learning, advocacy, or regional collaboration.
