About Us

Feminist climate justice, rooted in care

We see girls, young women, and communities affected by inequality and climate change as leaders, knowledge holders, and agents of change. Our work brings training, mentoring, and collective care together to support their leadership across the Caribbean.

Discover GirlsCARE

  • Our purpose

    Train, mentor, and support girls and young women to advocate for climate-smart and gender-just solutions.

  • Our goal

    Support the next generation of climate leaders who will drive sustainable development across the region.

  • Approach

    Climate justice education, feminist analysis, mentorship, storytelling, community care, and leadership development.

  • Values

    Feminism, climate justice, collective care, inclusion, human rights, and collective power.

Why GirlsCARE

Why this movement exists

Girls and young women from climate-vulnerable communities are often underrepresented in climate decision-making. We support their agency, knowledge, and leadership in shaping the response.

  • The gap

    Structural inequalities shape whose experiences are heard and whose priorities inform climate decisions.

  • Why girls & young women

    Girls and young women are knowledge holders and agents of change, not simply recipients of support. Their leadership matters.

  • Why climate

    Climate justice connects to livelihoods, food security, safety, health, and community resilience. It cannot be separated from gender justice.

The Coordinators

Meet the women leading GirlsCARE

GirlsCARE is guided by young feminist climate leaders working across Jamaica, the Caribbean, and global youth networks.

Ayesha Constable

Ayesha researches and publishes on gender and climate change through her doctoral studies, connecting agriculture, youth, and gender. She is a Global Advisor to FRIDA – the Young Feminist Fund.

Read full bio

She has served on several regional and global youth networks — including the Caribbean Youth Environment Network and Commonwealth Youth Climate Network — is former Caribbean Coordinator for the Sustainable Development Solutions Youth Network, and a regional representative for Global Power Shift (350.org).

Jhannel Tomlinson

Jhannel is a PhD candidate researching community-based adaptation to climate change, and cofounder of GirlsCARE Jamaica and Young People for Action on Climate Change, Jamaica.

Read full bio

She is Caribbean Advisor for the NextGen Climate Board, Sustainability Lead for the JAWiC Board, and the Prime Minister's Youth Awardee for Environmental Protection (2019). She has been named one of "50 Next" youth advancing the future of food and has represented Jamaica as a youth climate champion in Paris, Poland, and Spain.

Programmes

Programmes building climate resilience

From mentorship to disaster response, our initiatives equip girls and young women to act on climate and care for their communities.

01

Flagship Initiative

Mentorship

Our Young Women's Climate Justice Mentorship Programme connects girls and young women across the Caribbean with experienced advocates and peers. Together, they deepen their understanding of climate and gender justice and strengthen their ability to lead.

What participants gain

Practical climate knowledge, confidence to advocate, and a network of people who can support their next step.

Explore the Mentorship programme →
02

Lend a Girl a Hand

Practical support for girls, young women, and communities, including hurricane relief and Back to School support. Care and dignity connect immediate needs with longer-term recovery.

Our focus

Supporting recovery, educational needs, and wellbeing. Hurricane relief is part of this broader programme.

Explore Lend a Girl a Hand →
03

Envisioning Resilience

Young women in Jamaica use photography and visual storytelling to document climate change in their lives and communities, bringing their experiences into adaptation conversations.

Impact

Connects women's lived experience with adaptation decision-makers through photographs, exhibitions, and dialogue.

Explore Envisioning Resilience →
04

Care Collective

Shared spaces for connection, reflection, and collective care, recognising that wellbeing belongs alongside feminist climate leadership.

Our focus

Care for ourselves, one another, our communities, and the environments in which we live.
