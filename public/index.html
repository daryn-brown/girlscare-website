About Us

A young feminist climate network

GirlsCARE — Girls Climate Action for Resilience & Empowerment — equips women and girls across Jamaica and the Caribbean to lead on climate adaptation and resilience. Through mentorship, advocacy, and community building, we safeguard the economic, social, and environmental rights of women on the frontlines of the climate crisis.

Meet our coordinators

Why GirlsCARE

Why this movement exists

Across the Caribbean, the climate crisis reaches women and girls first and hardest — yet they are too often left out of the decisions that shape their future. GirlsCARE exists to change that.

  • The gap

    Young women live on the frontlines of climate impacts but remain under-represented in climate leadership. We help close that gap.

  • Why girls & young women

    When girls lead, whole communities grow more resilient. Investing in their power is one of the most effective climate strategies there is.

  • Why climate

    Hurricanes, floods, and rising seas threaten Caribbean livelihoods. For us, climate justice and gender justice are inseparable.

The Coordinators

Meet the women leading GirlsCARE

GirlsCARE is guided by young feminist climate leaders working across Jamaica, the Caribbean, and global youth networks.

Ayesha Constable

Ayesha has researched and published on gender and climate change as part of her doctoral studies, linking her interests in agriculture, youth, and gender. She has served on several regional and global youth networks — including the Caribbean Youth Environment Network and Commonwealth Youth Climate Network — is former Caribbean Coordinator for the Sustainable Development Solutions Youth Network, a regional representative for Global Power Shift (350.org), and a Global Advisor to FRIDA – the Young Feminist Fund.

Jhannel Tomlinson

Jhannel Tomlinson is a PhD candidate whose research focuses on community-based adaptation to climate change. She is cofounder of Young People for Action on Climate Change, Jamaica and of GirlsCARE Jamaica, Caribbean Advisor for the NextGen Climate Board, Sustainability Lead for the JAWiC Board, and the Prime Minister's Youth Awardee for Environmental Protection (2019). She has been named one of "50 Next" youth advancing the future of food and has represented Jamaica as a youth climate champion in Paris, Poland, and Spain.

Programmes

Programmes building climate resilience

From mentorship to disaster response, our initiatives equip girls and young women to act on climate and care for their communities.

01

Flagship Initiative

Mentorship

Our flagship, young feminist-led programme empowers women and girls as advocates for climate action — safeguarding the economic, social, and environmental rights of women through adaptation and resilience building, nationally and regionally. It runs through mentorship, technical presentations, network-building sessions, report and reflection writing, and virtual activism.

02

Hurricane Relief

When hurricanes strike the Caribbean, GirlsCARE mobilises to support women and girls — coordinating relief, care, and recovery for the communities hit hardest.

03

Envisioning Resilience

A creative space where girls imagine and design climate-resilient futures for their communities through storytelling, research, and reflection.

04

Care Collective

Our peer network connecting young women across the region for solidarity, knowledge-sharing, and collective climate advocacy.
