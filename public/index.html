Why GirlsCARE?

Climate justice needs the girls already living it.

Caribbean girls and young women are often closest to the realities of climate disruption, yet too often they are missing from the rooms where solutions are funded, designed, and decided.

GirlsCARE was created to close that gap through mentorship, feminist climate education, community care, and opportunities for young women to lead in public, local, and regional spaces.
