Participant story

Jamila Falak's continuing journey

Envisioning Resilience · Jamaica

Through Envisioning Resilience, Jamila developed photography and storytelling skills to document climate change and resilience from the perspective of a young Jamaican woman.

Her participation became part of a wider professional and advocacy journey. She subsequently pursued a Climate Justice fellowship with Climate Tracker Caribbean and later received a Chevening scholarship for postgraduate study in the UK.
