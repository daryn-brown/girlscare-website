Choose your path

Four distinct ways to take part

Each pathway has a different purpose, audience, and interest form. Share how you would like to take part and GirlsCARE will follow up when the right opportunity is available.

01

For girls and young women

Join the GirlsCARE network

Connect with peers across Jamaica and the Caribbean who care about climate justice, community resilience, and feminist leadership.

This path can include

  • Opportunities to learn with other young climate leaders
  • Invitations to eligible programmes and network activities
  • Regional connection, peer exchange, and collective action
Network interest registration is open.

Introduce yourself and tell GirlsCARE what you hope to learn, share, or contribute through the network.

Register your interest ↗
02

For people ready to contribute

Volunteer your time and skills

Support a defined programme, event, campaign, or relief effort where your experience can meet a real need.

Volunteer roles may support

  • Programme delivery, events, and community activities
  • Communications, documentation, research, or design
  • Relief coordination and practical community care
The volunteer interest form is open.

Share your skills, location, and availability. Submitting the form does not guarantee placement; GirlsCARE will follow up when a suitable opportunity is available.

Share volunteer interest ↗
03

For experienced guides

Become a mentor

Help a young woman turn climate knowledge and lived experience into confidence, connection, and practical leadership.

Mentors can help participants

  • Set goals and navigate a climate leadership journey
  • Build practical, professional, and communication skills
  • Connect learning to community action and next steps
The mentor interest form is open.

Share your experience, areas of expertise, and availability. GirlsCARE will follow up when programme dates, expectations, and safeguarding requirements are confirmed.

Share mentor interest ↗
04

For funders, institutions, and allies

Partner with GirlsCARE

Work with a young feminist-led Caribbean organisation on programmes, research, learning, advocacy, or community response.

Partnership can look like

  • Funding or co-designing a programme or regional initiative
  • Sharing technical expertise, platforms, or learning resources
  • Supporting research, advocacy, or rapid community response
Partnership enquiries are open.

Tell GirlsCARE about your organisation and the programme, funding, research, advocacy, or community collaboration you would like to explore.

Start a partnership enquiry ↗
