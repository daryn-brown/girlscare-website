Our flagship programme

Young Women's Climate Justice Mentorship Programme

A space for girls and young women across the Caribbean to deepen their knowledge, build relationships, and lead climate action with their own voices.

Leadership, rooted in justice

Young women are leaders, not beneficiaries

Our Young Women's Climate Justice Mentorship Programme brings together climate justice education, feminist analysis, mentoring, and leadership development. We support girls and young women to advocate for climate-smart and gender-just solutions, grounded in the realities of their communities.

Rather than treating young women as passive recipients of training, we create space for them to learn from experienced climate and gender justice advocates, build relationships with peers, develop their own perspectives, and strengthen their ability to lead.

The programme is deliberately Caribbean and intergenerational. Relationships and shared knowledge extend beyond a single programme cycle, helping individual leadership grow into collective power.

Who it supports

Lived experience belongs in climate leadership

The programme supports girls and young women interested in or already engaged in climate action, advocacy, community organising, and social justice.

We intentionally seek to reach young women from communities experiencing climate vulnerability, whose perspectives are often underrepresented in climate decision-making. Their knowledge, priorities, and leadership help shape the response.

We start with people, not projects

Climate justice connects to everyday realities: livelihoods, hurricanes, food security, safety, health, land, water, and community resilience.

Our approach

Learn together. Lead together.

Knowledge, relationships, and collective care are connected parts of our approach to sustainable climate leadership.

  • 01

    Learn

    Deepen understanding of climate change, climate justice, and gender justice. Connect wider climate conversations with lived experience and locally grounded solutions.

  • 02

    Connect

    Learn with experienced advocates and peers across the Caribbean. Build relationships of trust, share knowledge, and create networks of solidarity and collective support.

  • 03

    Lead

    Strengthen advocacy, communication, and organising skills. Develop an independent perspective and bring it into climate conversations and decision-making spaces.

Care is part of the work, not an optional extra. We connect leadership with the relationships and wellbeing that help people sustain their advocacy.

A Caribbean network

Different islands. Shared possibilities.

Rooted in Jamaica, the programme has connected young women, mentors, climate justice leaders, and feminist advocates across the region. By 2025, it was in its fifth year.

First cohort
15 young women
From Jamaica, Cayman Islands, Belize, Antigua and Barbuda, and St Vincent and the Grenadines.
A subsequent programme
25 young women
Reached across the Caribbean as the mentorship platform continued to develop.
More recent participants
15–27 years old
Young women from Jamaica, The Bahamas, Trinidad and Tobago, Barbados, and Guyana.

These are historical programme snapshots, not a cumulative participant total or current application criteria.

What changes

From individual growth to collective power

The programme is designed to strengthen participants' capacity to continue their climate advocacy and amplify their collective voices.

  • Deeper climate justice knowledge

    Understanding the connections between climate change, gender justice, rights, and the realities of everyday life.

  • Skills to organise and advocate

    Strengthening communication, advocacy, organising, and leadership skills that support continued climate action.

  • Relationships across the region

    Connecting with mentors and peers whose experience, perspectives, and encouragement support the next step.

  • Solidarity and collective support

    Building networks where people can learn from one another, share resources, and work towards change together.

  • A stronger voice in climate conversations

    Developing and communicating independent perspectives, with greater confidence to participate in advocacy and decision-making.

  • Pathways to keep growing

    Identifying opportunities for further learning, advocacy, and professional development beyond a single programme cycle.
