Care Collective

Care belongs alongside activism and leadership. Through connection, reflection, and shared spaces, we place the wellbeing of people and communities at the heart of the work.

Care is part of the work

Sustaining the people who make change

Care Collective brings GirlsCARE's commitment to collective care into shared spaces for connection and reflection. We recognise care as fundamental to sustainable activism and leadership, not an optional wellness activity after the work is done.

Within GirlsCARE's work with girls and young women, that means attending to the people doing the work and the communities they are part of. Leadership cannot be separated from the relationships and wellbeing that help sustain it.

More than care in a crisis

Our understanding of care includes emotional, psychological, social, and material well-being. It is an ongoing organisational and movement practice, not only a response when a crisis occurs.

Our care approach

Care for ourselves, each other, and our world

Collective care connects our own wellbeing with the wellbeing of our communities and the environments in which we live. Three connected practices guide this approach.

  • 01

    Rest and connection

    Recognising the importance of rest, wellbeing, and connection, alongside the demands of activism and community leadership.

  • 02

    Learning and reflection

    Learning from lived experience and one another, reflecting on what works and what does not, and sharing knowledge that can strengthen our work.

  • 03

    Trust and solidarity

    Building relationships over time, caring for one another, and recognising that collective power grows through connection and shared support.

Shared spaces

Time together, space to reflect

Care Collective activities have included the Q2 2026 quarterly session and the Feminist Collective Care & 2026 Visioning Retreat in Treasure Beach, St Elizabeth.

These shared spaces reflect our commitment to relationships, collective learning, and the people behind the movement.

Why care matters

Leadership that makes room for people

Our approach asks not only what we want to change, but how to sustain the people doing the changing. These commitments connect care to the wider work of climate justice.

  • Sustainable leadership

    Place people's wellbeing alongside their advocacy and leadership, rather than treating care as something separate from the work.

  • Collective power

    Connect individual leadership with relationships, solidarity, shared resources, and opportunities to learn and organise together.

  • Ongoing learning

    Make reflection and knowledge sharing part of how communities and movements continue to grow.

  • Resilience beyond a crisis

    Recognise emotional, psychological, social, and material wellbeing as connected parts of community resilience.

Care also connects to our climate justice mentorship programme, where relationships and collective support accompany young women's leadership.

Part of a wider commitment

Keeping care at the centre

Strengthening care and wellbeing is a current and emerging priority across GirlsCARE. Our wider organisational priorities recognise the need for emotional wellbeing, connection, rest, healing, and psychosocial support, particularly for women and girls experiencing climate-related shocks.

Care Collective sits within this broader commitment to feminist climate leadership, community resilience, and care. The aim is to sustain both the people doing the work and the people we serve.
