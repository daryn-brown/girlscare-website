Programme Impact

What change looks like in practice

Each initiative meets a different need while building the same foundation: informed, connected, and resilient young women.

Flagship programme

Mentorship

Who it supports: Girls and young women interested in or already engaged in climate action, advocacy, community organising, and social justice, including those whose perspectives are underrepresented in climate decision-making.

1

Learn

Understand climate change, resilience, and rights in everyday language.

2

Connect

Work with a mentor and meet peers facing similar challenges.

3

Act

Practise speaking and writing, then shape an idea for community action.

The programme strengthens climate justice knowledge, advocacy skills, and relationships that support young women's leadership.

Practical support and recovery

Lend a Girl a Hand

Hurricane relief is part of Lend a Girl a Hand, alongside work such as the Back to School Support Initiative. The broader programme connects practical support with care and the longer-term needs of girls and communities.

Programme focus

  • Hurricane relief and community support
  • Back to School and educational support
  • Dignity, wellbeing, and longer-term recovery

Visual storytelling in Jamaica

Envisioning Resilience

Working with the NAP Global Network and Lensational, GirlsCARE supports young women in Jamaica to develop photography and storytelling skills and bring lived experience into adaptation conversations.

Impact focus

  • Practical photography and visual storytelling skills
  • Women's own accounts of climate impacts and resilience
  • Exhibitions and dialogue with adaptation decision-makers

Regional solidarity

Care Collective

The collective connects young women across the region to exchange knowledge, care for one another, and advocate together.

Impact focus

  • Peer relationships across communities and borders
  • Shared knowledge and collective learning
  • A stronger regional voice for climate justice

Regional reach
