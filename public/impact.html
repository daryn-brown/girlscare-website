Programme Impact

What change looks like in practice

Each initiative meets a different need while building the same foundation: informed, connected, and resilient young women.

Flagship programme

Mentorship

Mentorship combines technical learning, relationships, reflection, and virtual activism so participants can grow as climate advocates.

Impact focus

  • Leadership and advocacy skills
  • Mentor and peer support networks
  • Opportunities to research, reflect, and take action

Community response

Hurricane Relief

GirlsCARE mobilises practical support after severe weather while keeping the needs and dignity of women and girls at the centre.

Impact focus

  • Dignity and sanitary supplies for young women
  • Rapid community care and relief coordination
  • Support that connects immediate needs with recovery

Youth-led futures

Envisioning Resilience

Girls use storytelling, research, and reflection to articulate climate-resilient futures grounded in their own communities.

Impact focus

  • Space for girls to define resilient futures
  • Creative and research-based climate learning
  • Locally grounded ideas shared with wider audiences

Regional solidarity

Care Collective

The collective connects young women across the region to exchange knowledge, care for one another, and advocate together.

Impact focus

  • Peer relationships across communities and borders
  • Shared knowledge and collective learning
  • A stronger regional voice for climate justice

Regional reach

Rooted in Jamaica, reaching across the Caribbean

Our programmes, partnerships, and participant networks connect local experience in Jamaica with wider Caribbean and global youth climate movements.
