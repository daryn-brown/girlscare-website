Privacy policy

How GirlsCARE handles website information.

GirlsCARE only asks for information that helps us respond to people who want to join, partner, mentor, support, or stay in touch.

If you contact GirlsCARE by email or sign up for updates, your information should be used only for that purpose unless you give clear permission for something else.

If donation tools, analytics, or a live newsletter platform are added, this policy will be updated to explain how those services handle information.
