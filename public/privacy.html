Before you share

Privacy and enquiries

How the website's interest forms work, the services that provide them, and where to ask questions about your information.

Updated 29 September 2026.

Using the interest forms

GirlsCARE uses Google Forms for network, volunteer, mentor, partnership, and donation-interest enquiries. The embedded Google Forms are loaded only when you open their panels; each also has a direct link that opens on Google in a new tab.

These forms support follow-up about the enquiry you choose. Submitting an expression of interest is not confirmation of membership, a programme place, a volunteer or mentor placement, a partnership, or a completed donation.

Google Forms manages its questions, validation, submission, and confirmation. Opening a panel or seeing a form is not confirmation that an enquiry has been received. Follow the confirmation shown by the form after submitting.

If an embedded form is unavailable or asks you to sign in, use its direct Google link or contact the GirlsCARE team. Without JavaScript, the direct links remain available.

Explore the participation pathways or share support interest.

The information you choose to share

Review the questions and instructions in the form before submitting. Share contact information and relevant details about your interest or proposed contribution, without including unnecessary sensitive information.

  • Do not include passwords, payment-card numbers, banking details, or identity documents in an initial interest enquiry.
  • Keep messages relevant to the pathway you selected; avoid unnecessary sensitive health or personal-history details.
  • If you are unsure about age-related consent or safeguarding requirements, confirm them with GirlsCARE before sharing personal information.

Programme requirements are confirmed by GirlsCARE. Historical participant ages shown elsewhere on this site are not eligibility rules for every current opportunity.

How enquiries are handled

Google Forms handles information entered into its forms. This static website does not copy or inspect the answers, and has no separate form-response database. Answers are not stored in the website's source files or GitHub repository.

Response access, storage, and notification settings are managed through the connected Google Forms account, not by this website's page code. For information about who can access responses, how long they are retained, or requests to correct or remove information, contact GirlsCARE.

No fixed response time is promised by these pages. Contact the team if you need to follow up on an enquiry.

Third-party services

Google Forms may process account and technical information under Google's privacy policy. The form's own access and sign-in requirements apply whether you open it here or directly on Google.

The website is configured for Firebase Hosting and uses Google Fonts. Some pages also contain OpenStreetMap maps. These services may receive technical request information when their content is loaded. OpenStreetMap's practices are described in the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

The Knowledge Hub's search and filters run in your browser; this site's search code does not send search terms to a server.

Donation interest is not a payment

The support form records funding or in-kind interest only. It does not take payment, complete a donation, or issue a donation receipt. GirlsCARE follows up with contribution instructions.

Do not enter card numbers or bank-account information in the interest form. The support examples on this site describe areas of work, not fixed-price packages or tax benefits.

Questions or changes to an enquiry

Use the existing GirlsCARE contact channel to ask about an enquiry or current information-handling arrangements: message @girlscareja on Instagram. Avoid posting personal information in public comments.

This page describes the website's enquiry tools. It does not replace the instructions on each form or confirmation from the team about your particular enquiry.
